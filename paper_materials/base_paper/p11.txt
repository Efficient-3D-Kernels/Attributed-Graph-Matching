VIII. REAL-WORLDMOLECULARVIRTUALSCREENING
BENCHMARK
To demonstrate practical efficacy in drug discovery cam-
paigns, Fast-WCS was integrated into an end-to-end virtual
screening pipeline and evaluated on two pharmaceutically rel-
evant target benchmarks from public bioactivity repositories:
1)Aromatase (CYP19A1):A key cytochrome P450 en-
zyme responsible for estrogen biosynthesis, representing
a prime therapeutic target for hormone-receptor-positive
breast cancer [23]. The benchmark contains 120 struc-
turally validated active inhibitors and 1,080 property-
matched decoy compounds.
2)Breast Cancer Resistance Protein (BCRP / ABCG2):
An ATP-binding cassette multidrug efflux transporter
implicated in chemotherapeutic multidrug resistance
[24]. The benchmark contains 95 validated active in-
hibitors and 855 decoys.
A. Evaluation Metrics and Screening Protocols
All molecular structures were loaded from 3D Structure-
Data Files (SDF), processed with hydrogen removal to re-
tain heavy-atom skeletons, and assigned chemical attributes.
Pairwise similarity matrices were computed against a known
reference active ligand. A Support Vector Machine (SVM)
classifier with an RBF kernel was trained via 5-fold cross-
validation.
Performance was evaluated using: (1)ROC-AUC(Receiver
Operating Characteristic Area Under Curve); (2)PR-AUC
(Precision-Recall Area Under Curve); (3)Enrichment Factor
at 1% (EF 1%)and5% (EF 5%), measuring the concentration
of true active hits among the top-ranked1%and5%of the
screened chemical library:

B. Screening Enrichment and 3D Structural Alignment
As detailed in Table III and Fig. 6:
•ROC-AUC and PR-AUC Superiority (Fig. 6a):Fast-
WCS Hybrid achieves an outstanding ROC-AUC of
0.884and PR-AUC of0.612on Aromatase screening,
outperforming ECFP4 fingerprints (0.741), Weisfeiler-
Lehman graph kernels (0.762), and rigid 3D shape align-
ment (0.730). Even without 3D geometric information,
the Fast-WCS 2D topological kernel alone achieves ROC-
AUC =0.792.
•Early Enrichment Factors (Fig. 6b):In industrial drug
discovery, wet-lab validation is restricted to the top1%
of candidate hits. Fast-WCS Hybrid achieves an Early
Enrichment Factor EF 1% =14.2×, meaning that the
top1%of the screened compound library contains over
fourteen times more active ligands than random selection.
This is more than double the enrichment of ECFP4
fingerprints (6.4×).
•3D Alignment Quality (Fig. 6c):Because Phase 1 iden-
tifies the exact maximum common pharmacophore sub-
graph, Phase 2 Kabsch alignment achieves tight spatial
superposition. Fig. 6c shows that active inhibitors exhibit
a sharp distribution centered at RMSD= 0.85±0.22 ˚A,
whereas inactive decoys cluster at RMSD= 2.45±
0.45 ˚A. Setting an RMSD threshold of1.5 ˚A cleanly
separates active pharmacophores from inactive decoys.
•High Screening Throughput (Fig. 6d):Fast-WCS pro-
cesses over115molecular pairs per second on drug-like
compounds (N≈30), enabling complete screening of a
10,000-compound library in under 90 seconds on a single
multicore CPU workstation.
