X. DISCUSSION, LIMITATIONS,ANDFUTUREWORK
The development of Fast-WCS bridges a longstanding di-
vide between theoretical combinatorial graph matching and
practical high-throughput chemoinformatics. By eliminating
numerical finite differences and polynomial relaxations, Fast-
WCS delivers a mathematically exact, strictly cubicΘ(N 3)
solver that preserves rigorous bijective correspondences.
A. Broader Implications for Chemoinformatics
Unlike black-box graph neural networks (GNNs), which
often suffer from over-squashing and lack verifiable atom-
level interpretability, Fast-WCS explicitly outputs the optimal
partial permutation matrixX ∗ ∈ PL. This provides medicinal
chemists with direct visualization of which pharmacophore
fragments align between lead candidates and target macro-
molecules. Furthermore, because Phase 1 outputs discrete
atom mappings, Phase 2 3D superposition executes inO(1)
closed form, enabling rapid scaffold hopping across struc-
turally diverse chemical series.
B. Limitations and Future Directions
While Fast-WCS scales efficiently up toN= 100on
single CPU cores, screening ultra-large combinatorial libraries
(>10 8 compounds) will benefit from GPU tensor acceleration.
Because all operations in Theorem 1 and Lemma 1 are dense
matrix additions and products, the entire Fast-WCS homotopy
loop can be executed in parallel on GPU tensor cores using
batch matrix multiplication. Future research will explore GPU
batching, extensions to multi-conformer 3D ensembles, and in-
tegration with protein-ligand binding pocket docking pipelines.
XI. CONCLUSION
In this paper, we presented Fast-WCS, an accelerated an-
alytical optimization framework for Weighted Common Sub-
graph matching in molecular property prediction and virtual
screening. By proving the Rank-One Factorization of the
co-matching indicator matrix (Lemma 1) and deriving the
exact analytical structural gradient (Theorem 1), we eliminated
the cubic tensor and quintic finite-difference bottlenecks that
hindered prior GNCCP formulations. Coupled with Frank-
Wolfe Duality Gap stopping certificates (Theorem 2), exact
line searches, and a cosine continuation schedule, Fast-WCS
guarantees strictly cubicΘ(N 3)asymptotic complexity and
delivers a>750×wall-clock speedup on macromolecular
graphs (N= 100). On real-world virtual screening bench-
marks, Fast-WCS achieves superior screening enrichment
(ROC-AUC= 0.884, EF 1% = 14.2), establishing an efficient,
mathematically rigorous foundation for modern computer-
aided drug discovery.
